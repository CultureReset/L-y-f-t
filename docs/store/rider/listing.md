## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Get where you're going.

Tap where you want to go, see what every option costs before you book, and
watch your driver on the map on the way to you. The price you're shown is the
price you pay — the receipt breaks it into every line that made it up.

BOOK IN SECONDS
• Set a destination and see prices for every class of service at once
• Add stops along the way
• Book now, or schedule a ride up to a week ahead
• Save home and work and book them in one tap

KNOW WHAT IT COSTS
• A quoted price, before you confirm
• Receipts that show the base fare, distance, time, and every fee separately
• Promo codes applied before you book, not after

RIDE SAFER
• Share your trip — driver, vehicle, and destination — with anyone you choose
• Emergency services one tap away, with your ride details on screen
• Check the plate, the car, and the driver's photo before you get in
• Message your driver without sharing your number

BUILT FOR EVERYONE
• Wheelchair accessible vehicles when you need one
• Tell your driver you're travelling with a service animal
• Ask for extra time at pickup and your driver waits

Questions about a trip? Every ride has help attached to it, and a real person
answers.
```
