## What this covers

The **Lyft Clone** rider app, the **Lyft Clone Driver** app, and the service
behind them, operated by Example Rides Ltd.

## What we collect, and why

We collect only what the service needs to work. Each item below maps to
something the apps actually do.

### Everyone

- **Phone number.** How you sign in. A one-time code is sent to it; the code
  is stored only until it is used or expires, and is never reusable.
- **Name.** Your first name and last initial are shown to the other party on a
  trip so you can find each other.
- **Email address**, if you give one. Receipts are sent to it. Optional.
- **Device push token**, if you allow notifications. Used to tell you a driver
  is arriving or a ride is offered. Removed as soon as the app is uninstalled.

### Riders

- **Location**, while the app is open. Sets your pickup point, shows the
  driver approaching, and prices the trip. We do not collect a rider's location
  in the background.
- **Trip history**: where you went, when, what it cost, and the fare's
  breakdown. Kept because you need receipts and because tax law requires it.
- **Payment method details**: the card brand and last four digits only. The
  full card number is handled by our payment processor and never reaches our
  servers.
- **Messages** you send to your driver during a trip. Kept for the period a
  safety investigation might need them, then deleted.

### Drivers

- **Location, including in the background while you are online.** This is what
  dispatch matches on and what the rider sees while you drive to them. It stops
  the moment you go offline, and a persistent notification shows whenever it is
  running.
- **Identity and eligibility documents**: licence, insurance, vehicle
  registration, and a profile photo. Required to drive, checked before
  approval, and retained as long as regulation requires.
- **Vehicle details**, shown to riders so they can identify your car.
- **Earnings and payout records.**

## What we share

- **With the other party on your trip**: first name and last initial, rating,
  photo, and — while the trip is live — location. A driver additionally sees
  the pickup and destination; a rider additionally sees the vehicle.
- **With our payment processor**: what is needed to take payment or make a
  payout. They are a processor acting on our instructions.
- **With someone you choose**, when you use "share my trip".
- **With emergency services or law enforcement**, when we are legally required
  to or when someone's safety depends on it.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers. Neither
app collects an advertising ID; the permission is explicitly blocked in both.

## How long we keep it

- Trip and payment records: as long as tax and transport regulation require,
  typically seven years.
- Location traces from a trip: retained with the trip, then deleted.
- Driver documents: as long as you are approved to drive, plus the retention
  period the local regulator sets.
- Verification codes: minutes.
- Everything else: until you delete your account.

## Deleting your account

In the rider app: **Help → Account → Deleting your account**. In the driver
app: **Help → Your account**. Deletion removes your profile, saved places, and
payment methods. Trip and payment records are kept for the period above and
then deleted. A trip in progress has to finish first.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export,
or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Contact
`privacy@example.com` and we will respond within the period the law requires.

## Children

Neither app is for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from
children.

## Security

Everything is encrypted in transit. Access to production data is limited to
staff who need it and is logged. We do not store full card numbers.

## Changes

We will post any change here and, if it materially affects you, tell you in
the app before it takes effect.
